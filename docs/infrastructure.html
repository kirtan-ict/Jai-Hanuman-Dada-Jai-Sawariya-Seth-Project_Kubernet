How to choose

Start from your constraints, not the technology

Most organisations do not pick an infrastructure type; their constraints pick it for them. Work through these questions in order and the options narrow quickly.

  1. Where must the data live? Regulation or contracts may rule out public cloud, or require isolation.
  2. What connectivity do sites have? No internet points to air-gapped; patchy links to edge.
  3. What do you already own? Recent hardware and virtualisation favour on-premises or bare metal.
  4. How big and experienced is the team? Small teams benefit most from managed services or a supported platform.
  5. What does the workload need? GPUs, low latency or very steady demand change the economics.
  6. Capex or opex? Budget model often decides between owned hardware and the cloud.

Mixing is normal. Many estates combine types: managed cloud for customer-facing apps, on-premises for core systems, edge for sites and a GPU pool for AI. That is what our hybrid practice is for.
Sometimes the answer is "not Kubernetes". A handful of services on a simple PaaS or a few VMs may serve you better. We explain when in our resources.
