The bridge to production-grade Kubernetes

Setu means bridge. That is the job: connecting your team, your applications and your infrastructure to a Kubernetes platform that is secure, observable and boring in the best way.

Our mission

Make reliable Kubernetes achievable for every team, on any infrastructure

Kubernetes is powerful, but the distance between a working demo and a platform you can trust at 3 a.m. is large. Many teams cross that gap alone, through trial and error, and end up with clusters only one person understands.

We exist to shorten that crossing. We bring proven patterns for cloud, data-centre, bare-metal, edge, air-gapped and GPU environments, build them with your engineers, and leave behind code and knowledge your team fully owns.

If Kubernetes is not the right answer for you yet, we will say so.

About us: FAQ

Are you partnered with a specific cloud or vendor?

Our recommendations are not tied to reseller margins. If we ever hold a partner status that could be relevant to your decision, we disclose it in the proposal.

Where do you work?

We are based in India and work remotely with teams in any time zone. On-site visits can be arranged for data-centre, edge or air-gapped work.

Is Kubernetes always the right choice?

No. For a small number of services with stable traffic, a managed PaaS, serverless platform or automated VMs is often simpler and cheaper. Our readiness assessment and decision guide help you check.

How big are your engagements?

Anything from a two-week fixed-scope audit to multi-month platform builds and ongoing managed support. Start small and expand only if it is working for you.
