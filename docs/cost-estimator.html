    Method

    How the estimate works

    • Compute: nodes × (vCPU × price per vCPU-hour + RAM × price per GB-hour) × 730 hours, less the spot discount on the spot share.
    • Environments: production counts in full; staging and development are assumed to be smaller copies.
    • Control plane: a provider fee per cluster on managed cloud, or the cost of dedicated control-plane nodes when self-managed.
    • Licences and overheads: per-vCPU subscriptions for enterprise platforms, supply-chain tooling for air-gapped, and GPU-hours for GPU nodes.
    • Storage, egress and support: flat illustrative rates per GB and a fixed monthly support fee.
