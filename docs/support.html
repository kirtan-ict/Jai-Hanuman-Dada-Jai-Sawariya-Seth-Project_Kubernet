Severity definitions

You set the initial severity when you raise an issue. We may agree to adjust it together as the impact becomes clearer.

No lock-in

You can leave at any time, with everything

We would rather keep clients because the service is good than because leaving is painful. These terms are part of every plan.

  • Your repositories: all Terraform/OpenTofu, Helm, Kustomize and GitOps code lives in Git repositories you own, from day one.
  • Your accounts: clusters run in your cloud accounts or your data centre; we access them with credentials you can revoke.
  • Open tooling: no proprietary agents or closed-source control planes that only we can operate.
  • Runbooks: operational runbooks, alert guides and architecture records are kept current in your repo or wiki.
  • Knowledge transfer: exit handover sessions with your team, included at no extra charge.
  • Clean exit: we remove our access and confirm in writing at the end of the agreement.
Onboarding

From signature to supported

Typical onboarding takes two to four weeks depending on how many clusters you run.

  1. Kick-off

    Agree contacts, escalation paths and communication channels.

  2. Access

    Set up least-privilege, auditable access to clusters and repositories.

  3. Baseline

    Audit each cluster and fix anything that blocks supportability.

  4. Monitoring

    Connect alerts to our on-call rota and validate with test pages.

  5. Go live

    SLA clock starts; first monthly report follows in 30 days.

Support FAQ

Why do you show “Custom quote” instead of prices?

Effort depends heavily on cluster count, node count and how many infrastructure types you run. We give a written quote after a short scoping call, and the quote always lists exactly what is included.

Do you support clusters you didn’t build?

Yes. Onboarding includes a baseline audit; if anything prevents us from meeting the SLA, we list it and agree a fix before the SLA clock starts.

Do you support managed services like EKS, AKS and GKE?

Yes. The cloud provider runs the control plane; we take care of everything around it — node pools, add-ons, upgrades, security, observability and incident response — and work with the provider’s support on your behalf when needed.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. You can move up a tier at any time and move down at the end of a billing period.

Do you cover application bugs?

Our plans cover the Kubernetes platform and its add-ons. We will help diagnose whether an issue is platform or application, and we can advise on application configuration, but fixing your application code stays with your developers.
