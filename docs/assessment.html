Kubernetes readiness assessment

Ten questions, about three minutes. You get a score out of 100, a maturity level, findings for each area and next steps. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to email the report.

Choose the answer closest to where you are today. All questions are required.

1. How do you run your applications today?
2. What Kubernetes skills does your team have?
3. How do changes reach production?
4. How do you observe your systems?
5. Which security controls are in place?
6. How confident are you in backup and disaster recovery?
7. How well can you see infrastructure costs?
8. How do you handle compliance requirements (e.g. SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, HIPAA, DPDP)?
9. How are environments provisioned and scaled?
10. Where do you want Kubernetes to run?

What we score

  • Team skills
  • CI/CD maturity
  • Observability
