How an engagement runs

Assess → Design → Build → Migrate → Operate

Five clear stages, each with a written output you sign off before we move on. Stop after any stage — you keep everything produced so far.

  1. Assess

    Interview your team, review clusters and pipelines, and score maturity across ten areas.

  2. Design

    Agree the target architecture, tooling, security model and success criteria in writing.

  3. Build

    Provision clusters and platform services from code, in your accounts, reviewed by your engineers.

  4. Migrate

    Move workloads in planned waves with rollback paths and agreed cut-over windows.

  5. Operate

    Run it with you under a published SLA — or hand it over with runbooks and training.

FAQ

Common questions

When should we NOT use Kubernetes?

If you run a handful of services with steady traffic and a small team, a managed PaaS, serverless platform or a few well-automated VMs may be cheaper and simpler. Kubernetes pays off when you have many services, frequent releases, multiple environments or portability requirements. Our assessment will tell you honestly if it is not the right fit yet.

Do you only work with one cloud or distribution?

No. We work across managed cloud services, on-premises virtualisation, bare metal, edge distributions and commercial platforms such as OpenShift, Rancher and Tanzu. We have no reseller incentive to push one over another.

What happens to our setup if we stop working with you?

Nothing breaks. All infrastructure code, manifests and runbooks already live in your repositories, and every plan includes an exit handover. See our no-lock-in terms.

How do we get started?

Take the free readiness assessment for an instant score, or book the fixed-scope two-week audit for a detailed review of your actual clusters and pipelines.

Can you work with our in-house team rather than replace it?

That is our preferred model. We pair with your engineers, review changes through your normal pull-request process and run enablement workshops so knowledge stays in-house.
